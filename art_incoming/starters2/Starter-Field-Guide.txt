FABLEMON: WILD ROOTS — STARTER FIELD GUIDE
Stages 2 and 3 evolve at levels 16 and 30. Movement rows from top to bottom: down, left, right, up. Each row has four frames. Battle sheets show front and rear views.

Leaf | Stage 1 | Samarrow
A playful seed spirit that hangs one-handed beneath a spinning sycamore seed. Its copper scarf streams behind it as it drifts over the hedgerows.

Leaf | Stage 2 | Samaslice
Now light-footed enough to ride the seed, Samaslice crouches over the hub and banks between branches by shifting its weight.

Leaf | Stage 3 | Samarazor
Armoured in seed husks, Samarazor rides a four-bladed propeller and carries twin sycamore sickles. Its autumn-coloured mane folds back as it dives.

Ember | Stage 1 | Wickid
A sly imp hides its grin behind a beeswax cape. The golden flame on its braided tail tends to give away its hiding place.

Ember | Stage 2 | Wickveil
Wickveil opens its wax cloak like lantern shutters. The curved golden lining catches the light from its tail, making the little imp loom large at dusk.

Ember | Stage 3 | Solwick
A living beacon crowned with amber reflectors. Solwick plants both feet and turns its wax mantle towards its tail flame, focusing the light through its great chest lens.

Tide | Stage 1 | Rillcurl
A cheeky turquoise river drake with webbed feet and two blue stripes. Its tail rises into a foamy wave, and it loves to tumble downstream inside the curl.

Tide | Stage 2 | Riptwake
Riptwake braces its forefeet against the riverbed and draws its tail around one shoulder. The hollow breaker shields it until it lunges through the open curl.

Tide | Stage 3 | Maelstromaw
An estuary guardian with a broad fanged muzzle and whitewater cheek fins. A whirlpool coils through its tail, ready to break forwards in one sweep.
